Phase 12: with how many of their own labels does Kev-4B reach Jev's zero-shot level on a matching dataset it has not seen?

Why this phase. Phase 10 (protocol/p10.txt) showed two things for a user who wants Kev instead of Jev on their own matching data. Public
labels from other datasets bring Kev-4B to Jev's level on some unseen datasets (abt-buy, walmart-amazon) and not on others (amazon-google,
dblp-acm). And with 2,000 labels of the dataset itself (the all arm) Kev-4B's F1 was above Jev's on all four, but that reading is inflated:
in those splits most test pairs have a record that also occurs in a training pair. The practical question is how many labels of their own
data a user needs, measured on records the labels never contained, with the recipe Kev's author documents for fine-tuning on one's own data.

Model: Kev-4B only (code kev-1.0, weights jaredpalmer/kev-4b@v1.0, base Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B-Base@1001bb4d). Jev (jev-1.13.0) appears only as
the comparison column, from the answers cached on 2026-10-05. No Jev output is used to train, fine-tune, calibrate or pick a threshold for
any model; every label is a human label of a public benchmark.

Data (experiments/database/p12_data.py; the four Magellan datasets of phase 10, files as frozen there)
- Every record of a dataset, as written in the file, is put on the test side or the label side by the parity of sha256("p12|<dataset>|<record>").
- Test pairs: the pairs of the test split, and of the 600 validation pairs Jev was asked (experiments/entity_match/run.py --sample 600,
  random.Random(0); Jev has no answer to the other validation pairs, and no paid interface is called), whose two records are both on the
  test side: abt-buy 632 pairs (59 matches), amazon-google 664 (66), walmart-amazon 669 (61), dblp-acm 766 (152).
- Label pool: the pairs of the training split whose two records are both on the label side (1,368 / 1,894 / 1,503 / 1,896 pairs, 146 / 183
  / 165 / 321 matches), in the order of sha256("p12|<dataset>|<pair id>"). No record of a test pair occurs in any label (p12_data.py check).
- Budgets n = 100, 300 and 1,000 labels: the first n pairs of the pool, so the budgets are nested. Following the author's recipe ("keep
  10-20% of the file aside"), the last 20% of each budget are held out to fit the temperature and the first 80% are trained on. The

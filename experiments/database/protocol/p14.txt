Phase 14: is Kev-0.8B's drop on movie reviews in phase 10 a property of seed 1 or of training without padding?

Why this phase. In phase 10 all five Kev-0.8B checkpoints (seed 1, trained before phase 10's rule-8 fix, so without the padding launcher)
were 2.0 to 2.4 points below the released checkpoint on the movie-review guard g1; phase 10 read this as slight forgetting. In phase 13 the
same arms trained with seeds 2 and 3, through the launcher, were 1.7 below to 0.3 above in all six checkpoints. Computed on phase 13's
answers before this phase (a reading made after phase 13, not part of its standard): over phase 13's three arms and both seeds, g1 is
+1.3 points (95% interval +0.5 to +2.1, review texts resampled) above phase 10's seed 1. The two differ in two ways at once: the seed, and
the launcher. Under one seed, the record order and which 2,000 decision-v7 records are replayed are fixed by Python's random module, so
padding leaves them unchanged; what padding changes is the shapes the GPU computes, which phase 10's probe found to move the trained
weights only at rounding level with dropout off, and to change the dropout draws with dropout on. Training seed 1 again through the
launcher therefore keeps everything of phase 10's seed-1 runs except the padding and its dropout draws.
Checked on the data alone before this phase (no model): the 2,000 replayed records of seeds 1, 2 and 3 contain 475, 513 and 465 records
of the three sentiment sources (yelp, imdb, sst5) and 138, 148 and 170 of amazon; seed 3 replays fewer sentiment records than seed 1 and
still passed, so the share of sentiment records alone does not explain the drop.
